🍱 About FoodBridge

Smart Food Donation & Distribution Management System

🌟 What is FoodBridge?

FoodBridge is a smart food donation platform designed to connect food donors, NGOs, volunteers and people in need through a single digital platform. It helps reduce food wastage by allowing surplus food to be shared efficiently with those who need it.

🎯 Our Mission

  • 🍽️ Reduce unnecessary food waste.
  • ❤️ Support communities facing food shortages.
  • 🤝 Connect donors and receivers easily.
  • 🌍 Create a sustainable food-sharing ecosystem.

⚙️ How FoodBridge Works

  • 🍱 Donors upload available food details.
  • 🔍 NGOs and receivers view available donations.
  • 🤝 Eligible users claim the required food.
  • 🚚 Food is distributed safely before expiry.

✨ Key Features

  • 🍱 Food Donation Management
  • 📋 Food Request System
  • 🤝 Food Claim Management
  • 📍 Location-Based Food Sharing
  • 📧 Email Notifications
  • 📊 Dashboard Monitoring
  • 🔒 Secure User Authentication
  • 📱 User-Friendly Interface

💡 Why FoodBridge?

  • ✅ Simple and easy food sharing process.
  • ♻️ Helps reduce food wastage.
  • ❤️ Supports needy communities.
  • ⚡ Provides faster food distribution.
  • 🌱 Promotes social responsibility.
